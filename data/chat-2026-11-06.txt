## 🌊 Natural & Scenic Highlights
- **Marina Beach** – 13 km long, one of the world’s longest urban beaches. Perfect for sunrise walks, kite‑flying, and street‑food stalls (idly, sundal, murukku!).
- **Elliot’s Beach (Besant Nagar)** – A calmer spot with a promenade, skate‑park, and the famous **Kotturpuram** lighthouse.
- **Guindy National Park** – A green lung right in the city, great for bird‑watching and a mini‑safari.
