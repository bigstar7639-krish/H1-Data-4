## 1️⃣ Primary School (Kindergarten → Grade 5/6)  
- **Goal**: Basics – reading, writing, math, science, social studies.  
- **Tips**:  
  - **Read daily** – story books, comics, even online articles (Tamil, English, Hindi, etc.).  
  - **Play‑based learning** – puzzles, math games, science experiments at home.  
  - **Language mix**: Tamil‑English (Tanglish) conversation helps build both vocabularies.
