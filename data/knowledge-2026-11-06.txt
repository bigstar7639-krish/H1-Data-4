## What is the Internet? 🌐
Internet‑na **oru massive network of computers** worldwide that talk to each other using standard protocols (like TCP/IP). It’s like a giant digital **pazhamudhir solai** where every device is a leaf, and the data flows like water through the veins.

- **World Wide Web (WWW)** – webpages (HTML) that we browse.
- **Email** – instant letters.
- **Social media** – Facebook, Instagram, Twitter (now X), etc.
- **Streaming** – YouTube, Netflix, Spotify – video & music on‑demand.
- **Cloud services** – Google Drive, Dropbox – files stored in the “cloud” (i.e., remote servers).
