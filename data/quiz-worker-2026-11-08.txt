Question: In the movie "Back to the Future," what speed does Doc Brown's DeLorean need to reach in order to travel through time?

Answer: 88 mph - Entertainment: Film. Itha kelvi Courage pathi - Answer 88 mph. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Courage pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Courage la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Courage oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
