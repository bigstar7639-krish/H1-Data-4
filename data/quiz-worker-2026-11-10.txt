Q+A Katha - Burger 🧠

Question: Which of these is NOT a map included in the game Counter-Strike: Global Offensive?

Answer: Oilrig - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Burger pathi - Answer Oilrig. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Burger pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Burger la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Burger oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
